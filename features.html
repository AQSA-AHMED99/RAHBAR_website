Pose engine

Real-time pose tracking without wearables

RAHBAR tracks body landmarks through a standard webcam, so patients never need expensive or intrusive hardware to exercise safely.

  • Camera-based landmark detection
  • Works with everyday webcams
  • Live skeleton tracking during every repetition

Analytics

Joint angle & range of motion (ROM) analysis

The system dynamically calculates joint angles such as elbow, shoulder and knee flexion, and compares them against therapist-configured targets.

  • Real-time flexion angles
  • Range-of-motion measurement per exercise
  • Targets configured by the therapist

Posture modes

Seated & standing rehabilitation exercises

RAHBAR supports standard standing poses and therapist-approved seated exercises, so patients with limited standing ability or balance can participate safely.

  • Dedicated seated upper-body poses
  • Standing exercise library
  • Clinician-approved exercise pool

AI model

AI / ML movement assessment

A machine-learning classification model evaluates whether each movement is correct, incorrect or compensatory, giving objective feedback in real time.

  • Random Forest movement classifier
  • Correct vs. incorrect vs. compensatory movement
  • Feedback for patient and therapist

AR overlay

AR guidance & voice feedback

Visual AR skeleton overlays, target markers and real-time corrections guide patients through every exercise, supported by friendly voice instructions.

  • AR skeleton overlay on the live camera view
  • Target markers for each movement
  • Voice cues for hands-free guidance

Storage & privacy

Structured data & privacy protection

Instead of heavy video files, RAHBAR stores lightweight numerical metrics — joint measurements, repetition counts and progress scores — protecting privacy and optimizing storage.

  • SQLite metrics storage
  • No raw video recordings
  • Faster, lighter record keeping
