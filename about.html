Our story

A rehabilitation platform designed around the patient

RAHBAR is an AI and Augmented Reality (AR) based rehabilitation system created as a Senior Design Project at Riphah International University in Islamabad, Pakistan. It is designed for rehabilitation centers, physiotherapists and stroke survivors.

Using only a standard camera, RAHBAR tracks body landmarks, measures joint angles and range of motion, and guides patients through therapist-approved exercises with live AR overlays and voice feedback — while clinicians keep full control of every plan.

Stroke rehabilitation is a core part of recovery care. The World Health Organization highlights early rehabilitation once a patient is medically stable, and RAHBAR aims to make guided exercise more available, measurable and engaging.

Mission & values

What guides our work

Patient first

Seated and standing modes make therapy possible for people with limited balance.

Clinical precision

Real-time joint angle and ROM measurements support objective progress tracking.

Privacy by design

Only structured numerical metrics are stored — not heavy video recordings.

Accessible tech

No wearables or special hardware: a standard webcam is enough to get started.

Technology behind RAHBAR

  • Camera-based pose estimation (MediaPipe landmarks)
  • Random Forest movement classification
  • AR skeleton overlays and voice cues
  • SQLite storage of numerical session metrics
  • PDF progress reports for clinicians
