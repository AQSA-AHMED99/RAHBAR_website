System capabilities

Engineered for rehabilitation excellence

Clinical-grade features built for precise therapy and confident recovery.

Real-time pose tracking

Tracks body landmarks with a standard webcam — no expensive or intrusive wearable hardware required.

Joint angle & ROM analysis

Dynamically calculates joint angles and Range of Motion (ROM) while the patient exercises.

Seated & standing exercises

Standing poses plus therapist-approved seated exercises for patients with limited balance.

AI / ML movement assessment

A machine-learning model classifies correct, incorrect and compensatory movements.

AR guidance & voice feedback

Visual AR skeleton overlays, target markers, live corrections and supportive voice instructions.

Structured data & privacy

Lightweight numerical metrics are stored instead of heavy video files, protecting patient privacy.

Clinical workflow

An integrated rehabilitation process

From registration to progress analytics — four simple steps keep clinicians and patients in sync.

1

Receptionist admin

Registers patient records and appointments before the clinical session.

2

Therapist control

Evaluates the patient and assigns tailored standing or seated exercises.

3

AR exercise session

Patient completes exercises with live visual overlays and audio cues.

4

Progress analytics

Structured joint measurements are stored for therapist review.

Why RAHBAR

Technology that keeps recovery accurate, accessible and private

RAHBAR combines computer vision with clinician control. It builds on MediaPipe pose landmark detection to understand body movement from an ordinary camera, so therapy guidance is available without special equipment.

The World Health Organization notes that stroke rehabilitation should start as soon as a patient is medically stable — read more in the WHO stroke fact sheet .

About the project

What makes it different

  • No wearables — just a standard webcam
  • Therapist-approved seated exercises for limited balance
  • Real-time AR overlays with voice corrections
  • Numerical metrics stored, not raw video
  • Exportable PDF rehabilitation reports
